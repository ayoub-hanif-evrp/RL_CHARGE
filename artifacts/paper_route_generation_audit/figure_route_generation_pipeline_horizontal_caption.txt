Compact route-generation pipeline. The upper row is the path from a SynthCharge instance to a frozen customer sequence and the later FA-HPPO charging controller. PyVRP solves a customer-only capacitated pickup vehicle-routing problem with time windows and does not decide battery charging. The charging-certificate search looks for one feasible charging trajectory; it is not a globally exact charging optimizer. Dashed branches are routes that are not retained. An unverified timeout or an exhausted search means no witness was returned and is not a proof of infeasibility. A route outside the frozen-route length bins, or a route whose layout, length, and charging-requirement quota is already full, is not retained. FA-HPPO chooses charging insertions and charge amounts on the frozen sequences and does not create or reorder customers.
