Route-generation pipeline in the order implemented by the builder. SynthCharge draws a source instance. PyVRP then constructs customer-only capacitated pickup-VRPTW routes and does not decide battery charging. Individual customer routes are extracted, and a route is eligible for the charging-certificate search only when it has 3–15 customers. A route outside that range is not retained and is not submitted to the certificate search. The certificate search looks for one feasible charging trajectory; it is not a globally exact charging optimizer. An unverified timeout or an exhausted search means no witness was returned. That outcome is not a proof of infeasibility. A certified route is labeled charging-required or no-charge-required, then retained only if the quota for its layout, length bin, and charge class is still open. A full quota cell means the route is not retained. Accepted routes are frozen. FA-HPPO later optimizes charging insertions and charge amounts on those sequences and does not create or reorder customers.
