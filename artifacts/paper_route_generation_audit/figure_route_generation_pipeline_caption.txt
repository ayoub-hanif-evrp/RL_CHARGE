Route-generation pipeline used to build the frozen customer sequences. SynthCharge draws a source instance. PyVRP then solves a customer-only capacitated pickup vehicle-routing problem with time windows and does not decide battery charging. The horizontal figure shows the order implemented in the builder: length eligibility is checked before the charging-certificate search. An unverified certificate search returns no witness and is not a proof of infeasibility. A certified route is classified by charging requirement and retained only while its layout, length-bin, and charge-class quota remains open. FA-HPPO later chooses charging insertions and charge amounts on the frozen sequences; it does not create or reorder customers.
